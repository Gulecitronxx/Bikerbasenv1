# Overall Impression

Bikerbasen's forside is functionally excellent (100/100/100/100 Lighthouse, 19 prior rounds of fixes) and its core differentiator — search by licence class, never guess — is genuinely well-executed and specific to the product. What's holding it back from "as nice as Bilbasen and modern marketplaces" is craft-level finish: a repetitive mid-page rhythm that buries the differentiator content in prose, dealer-branded photos that visibly clutter over half the featured grid, and small unbalanced/undifferentiated details (grid last-rows, badge legends, loading states) that a bespoke, cared-for marketplace wouldn't ship. The single biggest opportunity: make the A2/A1 rule *visual* (a diagram, not four paragraphs) and give the mid-page sections distinct rhythm — that's where the product's real advantage currently reads as generic.

# What's Working

1. **The licence-class filter is a first-class UI citizen.** Real radio inputs styled as pills, its own fieldset, hint microcopy and reset affordance, sitting as the largest visual commitment in the hero after the headline — most teams would ship this as a buried dropdown.
2. **The badge-fill convention encodes a real competitive fact, not decoration.** Filled "Mulig A1/A2" vs. outlined "Kørekort A" signals the exact thing a car-first competitor structurally can't offer. Argued-over, not default.
3. **"Ikke oplyst" is executed all the way down, including failure states.** Cards show missing data as missing and the hero explains how many listings a filter excluded and why — most aggregators would quietly drop or estimate to make cards look fuller.

# Priority Issues

**[P0] Mid-page sameness buries the actual differentiator**
- **What**: Four consecutive sections (problem → solution → trust → how-it-works) share one h2-plus-card-grid template (icon/number + heading + paragraph, 3-4 per row) with no variation in layout, background, or imagery.
- **Why it matters**: This is exactly the content meant to differentiate Bikerbasen (the kW/kg explanation, the two-tier "Mulig" distinction) — and it's the content most likely to be skimmed past because nothing in the visual rhythm says "slow down, this part is different."
- **Fix**: Give the A2/A1 rule a real diagram (kW, kg, derivation) instead of four paragraphs; vary background/layout between the four sections so the page has visible rhythm instead of four repeats of one grid.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`, then `/impeccable clarify` for the rule copy itself.

**[P1] Dealer watermarks obscure most of the featured grid**
- **What**: Confirmed by both assessments, cross-viewport: 5 of 8 cards in the "Motorcykler til salg" grid ("Sym XS 125", "Honda CB 1000", "Kawasaki ZR-7", "Aprilia RSV 1000" — all MC Syd; "Harley-Davidson FLHTCUI" — Rydbergs MC) have a large dealer wordmark/logo baked into the source photo, covering 40-60% of the visible thumbnail. Reproduces identically on mobile (390px).
- **Why it matters**: On first look this reads as "which site am I on?" and across a grid of 8 it's visual clutter competing with Bikerbasen's own compare-icon badge in the same corner. It's also the clearest "not built specifically" tell on the whole page — a bespoke marketplace controls its own photography; an aggregator inherits this.
- **Why this is hard, not lazy**: CLAUDE.md rule 2 forbids re-hosting or reprocessing source images beyond one thumbnail — cropping/blurring the source watermark risks crossing into "modifying the source's content." This needs a product decision, not just a CSS fix.
- **Fix (design-safe options)**: a subtle corner vignette/gradient mask (cosmetic only, doesn't alter the photo), or lean into it with Bikerbasen's own "kilde: mcsyd.dk" chip in one fixed, consistent position/z-index so the eye has one authoritative badge location instead of two competing marks.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable polish`.

**[P1] "Mulig" reads as confirmed at a skim**
- **What**: "Mulig A2" (possible, not confirmed) and "Kørekort A" (a hard, confirmed exclusion) sit in visually similar chip positions/sizes. The only cue distinguishing tentative from definite is the word "Mulig" plus a fill/outline convention explained nowhere on the page.
- **Why it matters**: This is the product's single most legally-sensitive UI element. A buyer skimming cards in a hurry can read "Mulig A2" as "this is an A2 bike" — precisely the harm the product's "we never guess" principle exists to prevent.
- **Fix**: A one-time, dismissible inline legend near the first badge encountered ("Mulig = ikke udelukket på de oplyste tal, ikke bekræftet"), or a distinct glyph (a "?" on tentative badges vs. a lock/check on hard exclusions) so the certainty gap reads without body text.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable clarify`.

**[P2] Unbalanced last rows in two grids**
- **What**: Browser evidence, both confirmed: the type-tile grid (7 tiles in 4 columns → row 2 has 3, left-aligned, one empty column-width gap) and the top-brands grid (12 tiles in 5 columns → row 2 has 2, left-aligned, three empty column-widths).
- **Why it matters**: This is exactly the finish-level detail that separates "as nice as a modern marketplace" from "functionally fine" — Airbnb/Vinted-class grids don't leave a lonely, left-stranded final row.
- **Fix**: Centre the final row, or reflow to a column count that always fills evenly for the current item counts, or add a "see all" tile to complete the row intentionally.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`.

**[P2] Peak-end undercut by a trailing SEO block**
- **What**: `slut-cta` (the emotional close) is followed in DOM order by `seo-browse` and several more facet-link sections before the footer — the actual last thing scrolled past is four columns of plain brand/type/region/budget links.
- **Why it matters**: Peak-end rule says the last moments disproportionately shape recall. Ending a legally-sensitive purchase-consideration journey on a link-farm-looking block undercuts the CTA's intended close, even though the CTA sits above it in the markup.
- **Fix**: Keep the SEO links crawlable and in DOM order, but visually de-emphasize them further (smaller type, muted colour, tucked near footer utility links) so the CTA reads as the human-perceived end of the page.
- **Suggested command**: `/impeccable layout`.
